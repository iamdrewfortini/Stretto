Focus – Idle – Switches/min – Input/min –

    How it’s measured

    Focus and idle are shares of measured episode seconds in the last 10 minutes. Switches/min counts episode boundaries. Input/min counts keys and clicks only, never their content. A summary of measured things, not a medical measure.

    Paths — your day as a route

    Collecting stops…

    Leg length is time: 8 px per minute. A detour is a stop under 2 minutes that takes you back where you were — like driving out of your way and rejoining the same road.
