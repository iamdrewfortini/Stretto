## What was deliberately not adopted

Vision/computer-use/robotics/image recipes (03/01, 03/03, 03/12–14, all of 05) map to no current Stretto surface:
Stretto never captures screenshots, so it has nothing lawful to send them. They are recorded here as read and
rejected on the product's own never-list, not as missing features. Multi-agent studio (03/08) and fanout (04/06)
run on the dev VM by house rule, not on this Mac; their tiering and isolation contracts are adopted above.
